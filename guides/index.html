Register Account

Desktop / Laptop

  • 1

    Click the Register button located at the right side of the main header navigation.

  • 2

    Fill in your required personal details, email address, and create a secure password.

  • 3

    Complete the process and verify your account via the link sent to your provided email.

Mobile Device

  • 1

    Tap the Menu icon situated at the top-left corner of your screen.

  • 2

    Scroll down and tap on "Register", then proceed to fill in your data and verify your email.

Purchase a Website

MyanCode offers powerful ways to acquire a professional website, depending on your business requirements.

Method 1: Custom Tailored Website

Build exactly what you need with dynamic, customized pricing.

  • 1

    Desktop: Click Packages from the main header.

    Mobile: Tap the Menu icon, then select Packages.

  • 2

    In the Packages interface, browse and add your desired custom designs, advanced functions, and total page count.

  • 3

    Submit your tailored request and our engineering team will handle the rest.

Method 2: Ready-Made Templates

Purchase complete, premium codebases that you can adjust and own entirely.

  • 1

    Desktop: Click Templates from the main header.

    Mobile: Tap the Menu icon, then select Templates.

  • 2

    Browse our catalog, select the template that fits your needs, and click Purchase to proceed to checkout.

Method 3: Professional Services

Request specific web development and custom services directly.

  • 1

    Navigate to the Services page from the main header or mobile menu.

  • 2

    Select your desired service, view the detailed description, and click the Purchase or Request button.
